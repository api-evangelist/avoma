# Avoma


Avoma is an all-in-one AI Meeting Assistant and Conversation & Revenue Intelligence platform that automates meeting note-taking, scheduling, coaching, forecasting, and CRM updates to boost productivity for sales, customer success, and revenue teams. It helps modern B2B organizations streamline meetings, gain insights from conversations, and drive revenue growth through AI-powered tools. Founded in 2017, Avoma serves over 1,000 global companies with secure, compliant solutions focused on efficiency and collaboration.

## Company Overview


**Avoma is an AI-powered platform that helps sales and customer success teams work smarter at every stage of the customer journey.**


It takes care of repetitive tasks while giving you the insights you need to close more deals and deliver better customer experiences.


Here’s how Avoma helps:


* **AI Meeting Assistant** – Get real-time transcription, instant meeting notes, and searchable recordings.  
* **Scheduler** – Automate lead routing and simplify calendar scheduling.  
* **Conversation Intelligence** – Track talk-time, topics, and call quality with AI-driven scoring and insights.  
* **Revenue Intelligence** – Stay on top of deal health with pipeline alerts, risk flags, and accurate forecasting.


Avoma integrates with your CRM, calendar, and collaboration tools so your team spends less time on manual work and more time driving revenue and customer value.




**Key Differentiators:**
- AI automation for meetings in 75+ languages with live transcription, instant meeting notes, and smart chapters
- Real-time coaching insights, call scoring, objection handling, and conversation intelligence
- Automatic capture of meeting notes, tasks, and CRM updates to ensure CRM hygiene
- AI-driven pipeline health scoring, forecasting, deal risk alerts, and revenue intelligence
- Smart scheduling, lead qualification, lead routing, and round-robin distribution
- 30+ native integrations with CRM, meeting apps, and productivity apps (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoom, Slack, Google Workspace)
- Compliance-focused security with SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, EU–US DPF, plus encryption at rest and in transit for enterprise-grade security
